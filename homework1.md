#  Technical Architecture Definitions

1. IT Infrastructure Diagram: shows the physical equipment that a company uses for its IT operations. For example it includes computers, servers, routers and network cables. It shows how these devices are connected and how they support the organisation's technology needs. This makes it easier to see what physical equipment the company has.

2. IT Landscape Diagram: gives an overview of the different IT systems and applications used in a company. It includes things like software, databases and the systems employees use to do their work. Instead of focusing on physical equipment, it shows the different IT assets and how they are related. This helps the company understand which systems it uses and how they fit together.

3. IT Architecture Diagram: explains how an organisation's IT systems should be planned and structured. It includes the principles, rules and standards that guide how the systems are developed and connected. These guidelines help ensure that the systems work together properly and meet the organisation's requirements. It also helps the company maintain a consistent approach when making changes to its IT systems.
